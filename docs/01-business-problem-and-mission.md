# Business Problem and Mission

PULSE pre-Azure review v0.1 · 2026-10-10 · Owner: Dr. Douglas Moore · Prepared for review

Fictional customer scenario; proposed design, not implementation or customer approval. Stop boundary: no Azure connection, provisioning, paid resources, site access or production release.

Mission: provide evidence-backed motor-bearing deterioration review so qualified maintenance personnel can prioritize investigation and approve work. Scenario: one plant, 12 motor bearings from a 60-motor fleet. Current process: calendar maintenance, monthly manual vibration rounds, historian exports and incomplete CMMS labels.

Reference scope: authorized public/local data qualification, baseline and candidate evaluation plan, reproducible lineage, explanatory evidence and a mock work-order review. Site scope is a future separately reviewed increment. Pump hydraulics, fan aerodynamics, gearbox diagnosis, electrical fault diagnosis, autonomous actuation and safety protection are excluded.

The 30% reduction in unplanned stops is a fictional outcome goal, not a model target or measured result. Target population exposure and causal attribution are unresolved. Use the [business case](../customer/05-business-case.md) rather than treating classification accuracy as ROI. The [cost model](../customer/04-lifecycle-cost-model.md) owns economic assumptions.
