# Concept of Operations and Stakeholders

PULSE pre-Azure review v0.1 · 2026-10-10 · Owner: Dr. Douglas Moore · Prepared for review

Fictional customer scenario; proposed design, not implementation or customer approval. Stop boundary: no Azure connection, provisioning, paid resources, site access or production release.

## Normal advisory workflow

Authorize input → validate contract and lineage → assess operating eligibility → calculate baseline/candidate evidence → publish advisory or abstention → reliability reviews → maintenance planner approves/modifies/rejects a mocked work request → record decision and subsequent outcome. Real CMMS writes and equipment commands are outside the reference increment.

## Degraded workflow

Missing/stale data, invalid units, corrupt artifacts or unsupported regimes produce unavailable/degraded status with reason. Previous healthy status must not be presented as current. Reliability returns to manual rounds and existing procedures. A reference sandbox is not a safety interlock; plant control and protective systems remain independent.

## Roles and concerns

| Role | Concern and decision authority |
|---|---|
| Dr. Moore, sole DME lead | Design, implementation planning, evidence collection and change proposals; internal review is not independence |
| Fictional plant sponsor | Scope, authorized commitment and acceptance |
| Fictional reliability engineer | Fault interpretation, warning usefulness and operating limits |
| Fictional maintenance planner | Work-order approval, scheduling and actual execution |
| Fictional IT/OT owner | Source access, network/identity/security and platform operation |
| Fictional finance controller | Cost assumptions, marginal loss and attribution |
| Future qualified independent reviewer | Consequence-based assurance when required; not appointed or claimed |

Review authority, competence, support hours and escalation must be verified for a real deployment. No stakeholder signatures are supplied by this portfolio exercise.
